## Relationship with upstream

Calivi is treated as an upstream source of useful fixes and ideas. Max is intended to become a self-contained project rather than a permanent collection of Calivi patches.

Important upstream changes may be selectively incorporated into Max when they are compatible with its architecture and goals.

## Porting policy

When significant upstream changes are incorporated after the fork, they should be recorded here with:

- the upstream commit;
- what was incorporated;
- any modifications made for Max;
- why the change was accepted or rejected.
